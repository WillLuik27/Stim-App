# Privacy Policy

**Pocket Stim**
Last updated: 10 August 2026

## The short version

Pocket Stim collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising,
and no network connection of any kind. Nothing you do in the app leaves your
device.

## What is collected

**No data is collected.** The app does not gather, transmit, sell, or share any
personal information, usage data, device identifiers, or diagnostics.

## What is stored on your device

Your settings — the haptic feel you have chosen, any custom tuning you have
dialled in, and your flash colour and on/off preference — are saved locally on
your iPhone using the system's standard preferences storage. They never leave
the device, and they are deleted when you delete the app.

## Motion

Pocket Stim uses your iPhone's motion sensors to detect when you shake or tilt
the device, so it can turn that movement into haptics and shift the lighting on
screen. This motion data is used only in the moment, on your device, to drive
what you feel and see. It is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

## Third parties

There are none. Pocket Stim contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics
frameworks, no advertising networks, and no crash reporting services.

## Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects nothing from children
either. It is suitable for all ages.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page
with a new date at the top.
