# Pocket Stim

A ball of goo that lives in your pocket. Pull it, stretch it, tear pieces off
and throw them around the screen — all of it running through the iPhone's
haptic engine, so the goo has weight and texture under your finger.

## Support

Something broken, or an idea for it? Email **william.luik@gmail.com** and
you'll get a reply.

When reporting a problem, it helps to include your iPhone model and iOS
version (Settings ▸ General ▸ About).

## Common questions

**I can't feel anything.**
Two things to check. Make sure **Settings ▸ Sounds & Haptics ▸ System Haptics**
is switched on. And Low Power Mode disables haptics system-wide on iPhone —
turn it off and the goo comes back to life.

**The screen flashes when I tap and I don't want it to.**
Open the gear in the top right and switch off **Flash on tap**. The app will
then never change your screen brightness or light the screen up.

**The haptics feel too weak or too harsh.**
Gear ▸ Change feel. There are four presets — Aggressive, Smooth, Melodic and
Off-key — plus a Custom option that exposes the underlying knobs: texture
spacing and rate, sharpness, jitter, the body of the tap, and the pitch line
the texture walks through.

**How do I throw a piece of goo?**
Swipe out from the centre orb. You don't have to drag it all the way clear —
swipe with any speed and a droplet tears off and flies. Sweeping a finger
across the middle keeps pulling more out.

**Does it work on iPad?**
Not currently. Pocket Stim is iPhone-only, because iPads have no Taptic Engine
and the whole app is built around what you feel.
